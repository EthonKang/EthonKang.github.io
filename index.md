## 👋 About Me

I am currently a Ph.D. student (since fall 2024) in Computer Science and Technology at the School of Computer Science and Engineering, **Central South University**, Changsha, China. Before that, I received my M.S. degree in Electronic Information from **Hunan Normal University** in 2024 and my B.E. degree in Communication Engineering from **Hunan Institute of Science and Technology** in 2020.

My research focuses on **intelligent sensing for industrial food processing**: building computer vision and near-infrared (NIR) spectroscopy systems that detect key quality indicators online — such as fermentation degree and withering moisture of tea — and taking them from the lab to real production lines.

## 🔍 Research Interests

- **NIR Spectroscopy & Chemometrics:** non-destructive moisture estimation, spectral sampling, cross-domain calibration
- **Computer Vision for Industry:** online quality inspection, image classification and detection, shadow removal
- **Wireless & Acoustic Sensing:** RF / ultrasound-based activity recognition and fire identification
